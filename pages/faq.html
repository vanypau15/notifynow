NotifyNOW, offered by Estate Resolution Technologies (UK) Ltd, is a service designed to streamline the process of notifying multiple companies of an account holder's passing simultaneously. This service alleviates the burden of contacting each company individually, thereby saving time and reducing stress for users.
NotifyNOW assits you in notifying companies and other service providers of the passing of an account holder. Our streamlined solution helps you to notify multiple companies simultaneously, removing the time-consuming and stressful process of reaching out to each one individually. NotifyNOW is a private, simple, and secure solution, designed to make a difficult time easier.
To register and complete our simple online form, you will need to have the following information available:
  • Proof of your identity (e.g., driver's license, passport).
  • Deceased's information (e.g., full name, date of birth).
  • Account numbers and email addresses for companies to notify.
